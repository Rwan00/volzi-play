Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what Volzi Play handles, why, and what we do not collect. Using the app means you have read this notice.

1. What We Do Not Collect

We do not require an account, ask for your name or phone number, run ad tracking, or sell data to third parties. The app does not upload watch links to a Volzi server for analysis or telemetry.

2. What Stays on Your Device

The video URL you enter is used only to play the file through the native iOS system player. Recent links are stored locally on your device with Shared Preferences so you can easily replay them. You can delete any item or clear the entire history list at any time from within the app.

3. Network and Content

During playback, your device connects directly to the media URL you entered. The third-party content provider may see your IP address and standard HTTP request logs according to its own servers. Volzi Play does not control that provider’s policies and does not inspect the stream as it passes.

4. Permissions Used

The app requires:

  • Internet Access: To load and stream your provided media files.
  • Wakelock / Display State: To keep your screen awake while watching video, preventing unintended sleep interruptions.

We do not request access to your Photos, Camera, Contacts, Microphone, or Location.

5. Children's Privacy

Volzi Play is a general playback utility and is not designed to collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian, you are responsible for the links opened on the device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if a new app capability affects data. The updated version will always be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our legal and support team:

Email: legal@volziplay.com or support@volziplay.com
