Frequently Asked Questions

Does Volzi Play support live M3U8 streams?

Yes. On iOS, Volzi Play utilizes Apple's AVPlayer framework, which natively supports HTTP Live Streaming (HLS / M3U8). Note that streams requiring custom HTTP request headers or DRM keys may not play.

Which video and audio formats are supported?

Volzi Play supports standard iOS formats including MP4, MOV, M4V, M3U8 (HLS), and audio streams like MP3, M4A, and AAC.

Why did my video link fail to play?

Common reasons include: expired video URLs, server geo-restrictions, unsupported video codecs (e.g. MKV/AVI not supported by AVPlayer), or unstable internet connectivity.

Are any videos stored on Volzi Play servers?

No. Volzi Play is a playback client. All streaming takes place directly between your device and the host server. We do not host or store any media.
